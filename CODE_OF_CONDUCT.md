## Our commitment

We are committed to a professional, welcoming, and harassment-free project for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

Constructive participation includes being respectful, giving specific technical feedback,
acknowledging mistakes, protecting private information, and focusing disagreement on evidence and
the work. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sexualized conduct,
trolling, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces
where someone represents the OptionChainAnalytics project. Maintainers may edit or remove content,
reject contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
behavior violates this policy.

Report conduct concerns privately to `artursepp@gmail.com`. Reports will be reviewed fairly and the
privacy and safety of reporters will be respected. Do not use public issues for conduct reports.

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
